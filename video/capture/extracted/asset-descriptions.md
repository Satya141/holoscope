# Asset inventory — Holoscope

All clips are real renders of the Holoscope app (frontend/, `?demo` mode, seed 7), 1080×1080, 30 fps, black ground,
glowing additive particles with bloom and a cyan holographic platform ring at the bottom. No hand/camera in frame.
Regenerate with `node scripts/render-clips.mjs`.

- `capture/assets/earth-reveal.mp4` (6.5 s) — planet Earth materialises from scattered particles (0–1.4 s), turns,
  then at 2.2 s cracks open north/south to the glowing inner core while the camera pulls back. THE hero / hook shot.
- `capture/assets/jet-reveal.mp4` (6.5 s) — turbofan jet engine materialises from scattered particles (0–1.4 s), hangs
  assembled and slowly turning, then at 2.2 s explodes along its axis into labelled parts (fan, compressors, combustor,
  turbines, nozzle, shaft, nacelle halves) while the camera pulls back. Montage (exploded tail).
- `capture/assets/car-open.mp4` (4 s) — sports car, assembled, then opens smoothly (0.5–2.1 s) into labelled parts:
  body shell lifts, wheels slide out, V8, gearbox, seats, suspension springs. "Open your hand → explode".
- `capture/assets/brain-point.mp4` (4 s) — human brain, slightly separated lobes; a gold dashed reticle targets the
  Parietal lobe (0.5–2 s) then the Temporal lobe (2–4 s); the target glows bright with a gold name label, the rest dims.
  "Point → inspect a part".
- `capture/assets/jet-pinch.mp4` (4 s) — exploded jet engine; a gold pinch ring grabs the magenta Combustor at 0.4 s,
  pulls it up and toward the camera (to 1.6 s), holds, then releases and it snaps back at 2.9 s. "Pinch → pull a part out".
- `capture/assets/earth-open.mp4` (3 s) — planet Earth with green continents and a satellite orbit ring; at 0.4 s the
  crust, mantle and outer core split north/south revealing the glowing inner core, with labels.
- `capture/assets/dna-unzip.mp4` (3 s) — DNA double helix (cyan & pink backbones, gold/green base pairs) spinning;
  strands unzip apart at 0.6 s.
- `capture/assets/car-spin.mp4` (3 s) — assembled sports car spinning fast, close framing. Montage filler.
